When questions require clinical judgment, medication changes, or review, AVELA prepares a structured, patient-editable handoff for doctor triage.

The Four Bounded Response Pathways

  • Care-Plan Explanation: Routine questions covered by current instructions cite verified clinician passages.
  • Clinical Review: Missing records or medication changes trigger an editable handoff summary for doctor triage.
  • Time-Sensitive Contact: Acute post-op symptoms (such as DVT calf swelling) route to on-call surgical liaisons.
  • Emergency Action: Red-flag presentations (crushing chest pain, stroke FAST signs) trigger immediate 112 in India call actions.
